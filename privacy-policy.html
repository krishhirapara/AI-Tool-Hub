Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains, in plain English, what information AI ToolHub ("we", "us") handles when you use this website. We try to collect as little as possible.

Information you give us

If you use the contact form or the newsletter form, we receive what you type in: your name, email address, subject and message (contact form) or just your email address (newsletter). We use this only to reply to you or send the updates you asked for. We do not sell your information.

If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address is stored by Cloudflare, the company that hosts this website, in its database service. We use it only to send you updates about AI ToolHub, and you can ask us to remove it at any time by emailing aitoolhub.co.in@gmail.com.

Messages sent through the contact form are processed by Web3Forms, a third-party form service, and delivered to our email.

Reader reviews

If you write a review, we store your display name, your rating, your review text, the tool it is about, and the date using Cloudflare, the company that hosts this website, in its database service. We do not ask for your email. We check reviews before showing them publicly; once shown, your name and text are visible to everyone. Please do not put personal details in a review. You can ask us to remove your review by emailing aitoolhub.co.in@gmail.com.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not use its own tracking cookies. It does use your browser's local storage to remember one small thing: whether you chose light or dark mode. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. The browser's local storage may also remember that a review was recently sent, only to stop accidental repeat submissions; it stays on your device. You can clear it any time in your browser settings.

Analytics

At the moment we do not use an analytics service. We may add one in the future to understand which pages are useful. If we do, we will update this policy and explain what is collected.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and sign up or buy, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. When you click such a link, the other company may set cookies or record your visit so it can credit us. Those companies have their own privacy policies.

Fonts from Google

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests the font files from Google, which means Google may receive technical information such as your IP address. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Third-party websites

Our reviews link to other websites such as the official sites of AI tools. We do not control those sites or what they do with your data. Please read their privacy policies before sharing personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete the details you sent through our forms, or to remove you from the newsletter, by contacting us. You can also clear your browser's local storage at any time.

Children

This site is made for college students and general readers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from young children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Please use our contact form or email aitoolhub.co.in@gmail.com.
