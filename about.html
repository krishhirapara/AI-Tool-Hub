About AI ToolHub

Why this site exists

There are hundreds of AI tools, and most guides are written for professionals with big budgets. AI ToolHub is different. It is made for college students and beginners in India who want tools that help with studying, assignments, presentations, coding and careers, without spending a lot.

The goal is simple: help you quickly find the right tool, understand what it is good (and not so good) at, and avoid wasting time or money.

How tools are chosen

We look for tools that are useful for student work, easy for beginners to start with, and have a free plan or an affordable way to try them. We also care about being clear on the downsides, such as usage limits or features that need a paid plan.

How tools are reviewed

Each review explains what the tool does, who it is for, its pros and cons, and a practical tip for Indian students. Prices and features change often, so we keep our statements general and always link to the official site for the latest details. Each review shows a "last checked" month, so you know how fresh it is.

AI tools can make mistakes. Use them to learn and to save time, but always check important facts, and follow your college's rules about using AI in assignments.

How this site earns money

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and sign up or buy, we may earn a small commission. It costs you nothing extra. Affiliate links never decide which tools we list or what we say about them. If a tool has a weakness, we will say so.
