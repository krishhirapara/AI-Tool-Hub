Overview

Perplexity works like a search engine that writes a short answer for you and lists the web sources behind it. That makes it handy for research, because you can click through and check where the information came from. Always open the sources for anything important, such as assignments or reports.

Who it is for

Students doing research who want quick answers with links to check.

Key features

  • Question-and-answer search with source links
  • Follow-up questions to dig deeper
  • Good for getting a quick overview of a topic
  • Web and mobile access

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Sources are shown, so you can verify
  • Faster than reading many pages for a first overview
  • Free plan available to try it

Cons

  • Sources can still be low quality, so check them
  • Free plan has limits
  • Not a replacement for reading your textbooks and papers

Tip for Indian students

Frequently asked questions

Does Perplexity show its sources?

Yes, it lists the web sources behind its answers so you can open them and check. The sources can still vary in quality, so judge them yourself.

Is Perplexity free?

It has a free plan with limits and paid plans for more advanced use. Check the official site for current details.

Can I cite Perplexity in my assignment?

It is usually better to cite the original sources it points to, not the AI summary. Check your college's rules on citations and AI use.
