Overview

Grammarly checks your writing for grammar, spelling and clarity, and suggests improvements. It works in the browser and in some apps. It is helpful for emails, assignments, cover letters and resumes, especially if English is not your first language.

Who it is for

Students who want cleaner, clearer English in assignments, emails and applications.

Key features

  • Grammar and spelling checks
  • Clarity and tone suggestions
  • Browser extension and app options
  • Useful for emails and cover letters

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Catches common mistakes quickly
  • Easy to install and use
  • Free plan covers the basics

Cons

  • Advanced suggestions need a paid plan
  • Suggestions are not always right for your meaning
  • Not a replacement for reading your work aloud

Pricing

Tip for Indian students

Frequently asked questions

Is Grammarly free?

It has a free plan for basic checks and paid plans for advanced suggestions. Check the official site for current pricing.

Where can I use Grammarly?

It works in the browser and in some apps through extensions and apps. Availability can change, so check the official site for what is supported.

Should I accept every Grammarly suggestion?

No. Suggestions are not always right for your meaning, so accept only the ones that still sound like you.
