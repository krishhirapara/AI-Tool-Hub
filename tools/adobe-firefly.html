What Adobe Firefly does well

Firefly is a family of generative creative tools. It is especially useful for generating and editing visual assets in an Adobe workflow, and Adobe also offers prompt- or image-based video generation. Feature access and credits depend on plan; inspect outputs and check current usage terms.

Best for: Students making or editing visuals, especially those already using Adobe apps.

Key features

  • Text-to-image generation
  • Generative image editing
  • Video generation tools
  • Integration with Adobe creative apps

Strengths and limitations

Strengths

  • Combines image generation and editing
  • Fits into Adobe creative workflows
  • Offers image and video tools

Limitations

  • Credits and features depend on plan
  • Outputs need review
  • Some features may have plan or region limits

Tip for students
