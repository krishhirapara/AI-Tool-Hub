Overview

Google Gemini is Google's AI assistant. You can ask questions, get help writing and brainstorm ideas. Because it comes from Google, it may connect with other Google services you already use. Students who live in Google Docs, Drive and Gmail may find it convenient. Features and availability can vary, so check what is offered for your account.

Who it is for

Students already using a Google account and Google apps every day.

Key features

  • Chat-style answers and writing help
  • Brainstorming and summarising
  • Coding help and explanations
  • May connect with other Google services
  • Web and mobile access

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Easy to start if you already have a Google account
  • Free access available with limits
  • Fits into a Google-based study routine

Cons

  • Some features may depend on your account or region
  • Can make mistakes, so verify facts
  • Free limits can change

Pricing

Can be used with a Google account on a free plan with limits. Paid options may offer more. Check the official site for current details.
