Overview

NotebookLM is a Google tool built around your own material. You add documents such as notes or readings, and then ask questions or request summaries based on what you added. This makes it useful for revision, because answers are tied to your sources rather than the whole internet.

Who it is for

Students who want to study from their own notes, slides and readings.

Key features

  • Ask questions about documents you upload
  • Summaries based on your own sources
  • Helps you find key points before exams
  • Works with a Google account

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Focused on your own material
  • Great for revision and summaries
  • Free to try with a Google account

Cons

  • Only as good as the material you give it
  • Features and limits can change
  • Still check its summaries against your originals
