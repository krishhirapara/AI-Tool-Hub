Overview

Canva is a drag-and-drop design tool with many ready-made templates. Students use it for project posters, presentations, resumes and simple graphics. It also includes AI-powered features, which may differ between free and paid plans.

Who it is for

Students who want good-looking designs without learning design software.

Key features

  • Ready-made templates for posters, slides and resumes
  • Drag-and-drop editor
  • Some AI-powered design helpers
  • Download your work in common formats
  • Web and mobile access

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Very beginner-friendly
  • Lots of templates to start from
  • Free plan available

Cons

  • Some templates and features need a paid plan
  • Many people use the same templates, so designs can look alike
  • Needs an internet connection for most work

Pricing

Has a free plan and paid plans with more features. Check the official site for current pricing and any education offers.

Tip for Indian students

Start from a resume template, keep it to one clean page, and export it as a PDF before applying for internships.

Frequently asked questions

Is Canva free for students?

Canva has a free plan and paid plans with more features. Education offers may exist, so check the official site for the latest details.

What can I make with Canva?

Students commonly make project posters, presentations, resumes and social media graphics using ready-made templates.

Do I need design skills to use Canva?

No. It uses a drag-and-drop editor and templates, so beginners can get good results quickly.
