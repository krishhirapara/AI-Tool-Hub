What Ideogram does well

Ideogram generates and edits images, with a particular emphasis on typography in graphics. Canvas tools support editing workflows such as filling, extending, and remixing. It is useful for poster and cover concepts, but generated lettering still needs proofreading and may need correction.

Best for: Posters, covers and social graphics where text is part of the image.

Key features

  • Prompt-based image generation
  • Typography-focused graphics
  • Canvas editing and remixing
  • Text layer editing tools

Strengths and limitations

Strengths

  • Useful for text-heavy visual concepts
  • Canvas supports iterative edits
  • Good fit for posters and covers

Limitations

  • Text is not guaranteed error-free
  • Plan limits and export options vary
  • Final precision may require design software

Tip for students

Include the exact headline in your prompt, then zoom in and proofread every word.
