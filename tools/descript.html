What Descript does well

Descript is an audio and video editor built around transcripts. It transcribes recordings and lets you edit media by changing transcript text, with AI-assisted cleanup and repurposing features. It is practical for interviews, lectures and podcasts; review transcription and edits, especially names and technical terms.

Best for: Editing interviews, presentations, tutorials and podcast recordings.

Key features

  • Text-based audio and video editing
  • Speech transcription
  • AI-assisted editing tools
  • Recording and media export

Strengths and limitations

Strengths

  • Transcript-based editing is intuitive
  • Combines recording, transcription and editing
  • Useful for repurposing recordings

Limitations

  • Transcripts need correction
  • Usage limits depend on plan
  • Not a text-to-video generator

Tip for students

Correct the transcript before editing, then check the export for unnatural cuts.
