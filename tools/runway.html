What Runway does well

Runway provides generative video models and creative tools. Depending on the chosen model, creators can generate clips from text or animate a still image. It suits concept shots and short sequences; generation limits, duration, and controls depend on the model and plan.

Best for: Prototyping short visual clips or animating still images.

Key features

  • Text-to-video on supported models
  • Image-to-video on supported models
  • Multiple tools and generation modes
  • Iterative creative workflow

Strengths and limitations

Strengths

  • Purpose-built generative video workflow
  • Can start from text or a still image
  • Several tools in one platform

Limitations

  • Generations use plan credits
  • Clip controls vary by model
  • Consistency across shots may take iteration

Tip for students

Use it for short illustrative shots and edit them together; do not expect a single prompt to make a complete explainer.
