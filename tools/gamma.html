Overview

Gamma uses AI to build presentations from a short prompt or an outline. You can then edit the slides, change the look and share them. It can save time on a first draft, but you should still check the facts and make the content your own before presenting.

Who it is for

Students who need a quick first draft of a presentation.

Key features

  • Generate a slide deck from a prompt or outline
  • Edit text and layouts after generation
  • Change themes easily
  • Share your presentation online
